Meet Dr. Vinaya

Chief Dental Surgeon & Founder

Dr. Vinaya Hudedavar is a highly skilled and compassionate dental professional dedicated to providing top-tier oral healthcare at Dantaveda: Smiles Studio. Drawing on extensive experience from her time serving in diverse clinics across Bangalore, Hyderabad, and New Jersey, USA, she is committed to delivering comprehensive and personalized patient care.

  • BDS - Bapuji Dental College & Hospital, Davangere
  • Certified - Dental Billing Specialist (CDBS) & Dental Coder (CDC)
  • Extensive clinical experience in patient care and treatment planning

Her approach to dentistry combines state-of-the-art technology with a gentle touch, ensuring that every patient—from children to seniors—feels completely at ease. She excels in clinical support, infection control, and providing clear post-op guidance.
